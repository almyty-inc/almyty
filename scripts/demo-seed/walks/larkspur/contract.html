Warehousing and Fulfilment Services Agreement

Agreement no. NGF-2026-0412 · Draft for signature, 14 October 2026

Between Northgate Fulfilment Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (no. 11873402), Unit 4, Trafford Park Logistics Centre, Manchester M17 1NG ("Provider")

and Larkspur Home B.V., registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK 81234567), Keizersgracht 210, 1016 DZ Amsterdam ("Customer").

1. Services

1.1 Provider will receive, store, pick, pack and dispatch Customer's products from its warehouse in Venlo, the Netherlands, and handle returns, as described in Schedule 1.

1.2 Provider will dispatch 98% of orders received before 14:00 CET on the same working day. If it does not meet this level in a calendar month, Customer's sole remedy is a credit of 2% of that month's fulfilment fees.

2. Term and renewal

2.1 This Agreement starts on 1 January 2027 and runs for 24 months.

2.2 It then renews automatically for successive periods of 12 months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days before the end of the current period.

3. Fees

3.1 Customer pays the fees in Schedule 2: EUR 0.95 per order picked and packed, EUR 0.18 per additional item, EUR 14.50 per pallet per month of storage, and EUR 2.10 per return processed.

3.2 Customer commits to a minimum of 6,000 orders per calendar month. For each order below that minimum, Customer pays EUR 1.10.

3.3 Provider may increase the fees once in each contract year by up to 9% by giving Customer 30 days' written notice.

3.4 Invoices are payable within 14 days. Late payments carry interest at 8% per year above the Bank of England base rate.

4. Stock

4.1 Title to the stock remains with Customer. Provider has a general lien over all stock in its possession for any amounts Customer owes to Provider under this Agreement, and may sell stock held under the lien 30 days after giving notice.

4.2 Customer insures the stock against all risks while it is in the warehouse. Provider is not responsible for loss of or damage to stock unless caused by its gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

5. Liability

5.1 Provider's total liability in any contract year, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, is limited to the fees paid by Customer in the 3 months before the event giving rise to the claim.

5.2 Neither party is liable for loss of profit, revenue or goodwill.

5.3 Customer indemnifies Provider against all claims, losses and costs arising from the products, their packaging or their labelling, without limit.

6. Personal data

6.1 Provider will process the names, addresses, email addresses and telephone numbers of Customer's buyers to dispatch orders and handle returns. Provider may use sub-processors, including its carrier partners, at its discretion.

7. Termination

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within 30 days of written notice.

7.2 Customer may terminate for convenience on 180 days' written notice by paying an early termination fee equal to 6 months of the minimum monthly fees under clause 3.2.

7.3 On termination Provider will release the stock once all invoices are paid.

8. Governing law

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. The courts of London have exclusive jurisdiction.
