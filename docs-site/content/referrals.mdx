# Referrals

Invite people to almyty and you both get free Pro time. Share your personal link; when someone signs up with it they start with 30 days of Pro, and you earn more Pro days as they start using almyty and when they become a paying customer.

## Share your link

1. Open **Settings → Billing → Referrals**.
2. Click **Copy** next to your link and send it to whoever you want to invite.

Everyone has one permanent link. The same page shows how many people you **Invited**, how many **Qualified**, how many were **Rewarded**, your **Reward days**, and a list of **Your referrals** with their status and the days each earned you. It never shows who the people are.

## What you both get

- **They get 30 days of Pro** as soon as they sign up with your link.
- **You get 14 days of Pro** when their organization is up and running: it has at least one gateway and has completed at least one agent run.
- **You get 30 more days** if their organization starts a paid plan. The free Pro trial does not count.

Rewards are checked every 10 minutes, so they arrive shortly after the milestone rather than instantly.

**Good to know:**

- Rewards extend how long your organization has Pro. There is no cash or credit.
- You can earn at most 365 days in any 365-day period. Past that, a reward is cut down to what fits.
- If your organization is on the free plan when a reward arrives, the days are saved and applied automatically once it is on Pro.
- Enterprise organizations cannot take part, on either side.
- If someone clicks several people's links, the last link they clicked counts. A link is remembered for 30 days.

## When a referral is held for review

Two things put a referral on hold: signing up with a throwaway email address, or signing up from the same internet address the link was made from (or as an earlier referral of yours). A held referral shows as **Pending review**. The new user gets no sign-up bonus and it earns you nothing until an operator clears it.

## For developers

- Share links look like `https://app.example.com/r/AB3D7XKQ` (an 8-character code); `/auth/register?ref=CODE` works too.
- The link sets an httpOnly attribution cookie, `almyty_ref`, for 30 days and redirects to registration. Attribution is last-touch; the cookie is consumed at registration.
- The `/r/<code>` endpoint is rate-limited to 20 requests an hour per IP. Appending `?format=json` returns `{"attributed": true|false}` instead of a redirect.
- Rewards extend the organization's `planExpiresAt`. Days banked on the free plan are `accruedRewardDays`; the total is `totalRewardDays`.
- Referral statuses: `pending`, then `qualified` (activation, +14 days), then `rewarded` (paid conversion, +30 days, final). Held referrals are `pending_review`, flagged `disposable_email` or `same_ip`.
- The same data is under `/referrals` in the [API reference](/api-reference/authentication).

**Configuration (self-hosting).** Environment variables on the backend:

| Variable | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `REFERRAL_REFEREE_DAYS` | `30` | the new user's Pro trial at signup |
| `REFERRAL_TIER1_DAYS` | `14` | your reward on activation |
| `REFERRAL_TIER2_DAYS` | `30` | your reward on paid conversion |
| `REFERRAL_YEARLY_CAP_DAYS` | `365` | rolling 365-day cap on your rewards |
| `REFERRAL_SWEEP_INTERVAL_MS` | `600000` | how often rewards are checked (10 minutes) |

`FRONTEND_URL` is used to build share links and the redirect after attribution.
