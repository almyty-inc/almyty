# Cross-vendor contract and risk review

**Stack**: `Trigger: upload / agent API` · `Models: Claude + GPT-4o + Gemini panelists, judge` · `Tools: clause/precedent search (optional)` · `Work mode: Panel` · `Output: risk memo from what the panel agreed on`

**Who it's for**: legal, procurement, and diligence teams where one model's read isn't enough.
**What it does**: several models read a document's risks from different angles and a judge writes the final memo, so a missed indemnity or auto-renewal clause is caught by the model that was looking for it, not left to one opinion.

## Build it

1. **Create an autonomous agent** and write its instructions: review the contract and write a risk memo.
2. **Set the work mode to Panel.** The slots follow: the main model, two or more panelists and an optional judge. Give each panelist a different provider, and under its **Advanced** a lens in its extra instructions: *tenant's risk*, *counterparty's leverage*, *compliance with governing law*. A panelist can also be one of your other agents, with its own tools.

   ![The Work mode section set to Panel, with panelists on different providers and a judge](/screenshots/agent-collab-debate.png)
3. **Add a judge**: the model that reads what the panelists said and writes the one answer they agree on. Without a judge, the main model does it.
4. **(Optional) give it a search tool** under **Capabilities > Tools and APIs** for your clause library or past contracts, so readings cite precedent, not just the text.
5. **Feed it the document**: upload the contract or have your DMS call the agent API.
6. **Get the memo**: one structured risk summary. The run shows each panelist's answer, which model gave it, and how far they agreed.

## Make it yours

- Reuse the pattern for research synthesis, due diligence, or incident post-mortems, anywhere one model isn't enough.
- Keep Panel for high-stakes calls; for high-volume, low-stakes work use **Cascade**, where a cheaper model answers first and the main model steps in only when a checker rejects the draft.
- Route anything above a risk score to a human via [`request_approval`](/approvals).
