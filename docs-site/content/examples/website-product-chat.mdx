# Product chat for your website

Put a chat on your website and campaign pages that answers product questions from your own documentation. A visitor asks "Does the Brew 2 grinder work in the UK?" and gets an answer taken from your help center, with the article it came from. A daily spending cap makes sure a busy campaign can never run up a surprise bill.

This guide builds it for Northwind's marketing team. It takes about 15 minutes and needs no code. The only technical part is pasting one line into your website, which whoever runs your site can do in a minute.

**What you need**

- An almyty account with an AI model connected. If you have not done that yet, follow step 1 of the [customer support guide](/examples/customer-support-assistant#1-connect-an-ai-model).
- A way for the assistant to read your documentation. Most help-center and documentation tools have an API (a way for software to ask them for articles) with a description file, often called "OpenAPI" or "Swagger". Ask whoever runs your help center for the link.

## 1. Connect your help center

In the sidebar, open **APIs** and click **Connect an API**. Pick **OpenAPI / Swagger**, paste the link to your help center's description on **Link** (or pick **File** and upload it), and click **Connect API**.

almyty works out what your help center can do. Here: search the documentation, and read one article. Each becomes a tool the assistant can use.

![The help center connected, with search and read](/screenshots/guides/marketing-1-connect-docs.png)

## 2. Create the assistant

Open **Agents**, click **Create agent** and pick **Autonomous** at the top.

- Click the name **New agent** and call it `Product questions`.
- **Work mode**, the first section: open **Model** and pick a low-cost model, for example **gpt-4o-mini**. Public chats get a lot of short questions, and the price per answer is shown next to each model.
- **Personality & style**: `Warm and clear. Short answers, plain words.`
- **Instructions**: tell it to stay within your docs.

> You answer questions from visitors to the Northwind website about our coffee equipment. Answer only from the help center: search it first, and name the article you used. If the help center does not answer the question, say so and suggest the contact page. Never promise prices, discounts or delivery dates.

Then scroll to **Capabilities → Tools and APIs** and tick **All tools** next to your help center.

![The product assistant, with the help center's tools ticked](/screenshots/guides/marketing-2-agent.png)

Click **Save**, go back to **Agents**, open **Product questions** and click **Activate**.

## 3. Try a question

On the **Overview** tab, under **Try it**, ask what a visitor would: `Does the Brew 2 grinder work in the UK?`

![An answer taken from the help center, with its source](/screenshots/guides/marketing-3-try.png)

The assistant searched the help center, found the article on power and voltage, and answered from it, naming the article. If your docs do not cover a question, it says so instead of guessing, because your instructions tell it to.

## 4. Set a daily spending cap

Open the **Channels** tab and click **Branding and visitor rules**. Everything here applies to every place you put the chat.

- **Look**: **Name people see** (`Ask Northwind`), **Brand color**, a **Greeting**, and **Suggested prompts** such as "Which grinder is right for me?".
- **Who can use it**: **Anyone with the link**, since it is for website visitors.
- Open **Advanced**. Under **What it may cost**:
  - **Spend limit per run**: the most a single answer may cost, here `0.10`.
  - **Spend limit per day**: here `10`. Once visitors have used that much today, the chat tells them it has reached its limit until tomorrow (midnight UTC), and owners and admins get a notification.
  - **Spend limit per month**: here `200`.
  - **Messages per visitor, per hour**: here `20`, so one person cannot use up the day's allowance for everyone. **Messages per IP address, per hour** does the same for visitors sharing a connection.

![Spending caps and message limits for visitors](/screenshots/guides/marketing-4-limits.png)

Click **Save**. The top of this page always shows what has been spent today and this month against your caps.

## 5. Put the chat bubble on your website

Back on the **Channels** tab, click **Add channel**, pick **Website widget** and click **Publish**.

- Under **Add it to your site**, copy the line with the copy button and send it to whoever runs your website. It goes on every page that should show the chat, including campaign landing pages.
- Under **Allowed sites**, type your website's address (for example `https://www.northwind.example`) and click **Add**. Only the sites on this list can show the chat, so nobody can put your assistant, and your spending, on their own site.
- Choose where the bubble sits and its icon under **How it looks**; the preview shows it with your name and greeting.

Click **Save**.

![The widget limited to Northwind's own website](/screenshots/guides/marketing-5-widget.png)

## 6. A link for emails, ads and social posts

For places where you cannot add code (an email, an ad, a social post), add a **Web chat** too: **Add channel**, **Web chat**, **Publish**. It is a chat page with its own link, with the same look, the same limits and the same answers.

![A visitor asking a product question in the web chat](/screenshots/guides/marketing-6-visitor.png)

## Keep an eye on it

- **Runs** on the assistant shows every question and answer, and what each cost.
- **Analytics** in the sidebar shows usage and spending over time.
- If an answer is off, fix the article in your help center: the assistant reads it fresh every time. To change how it answers, edit its **Instructions**.
- Visitors can download or delete their own chat, and chats are deleted after the period you set under **Visitor data**. See [Hosted chat](/hosted-chat).

## For developers

- This walk-through is automated in `scripts/demo-seed/guides.mjs marketing` on the local demo stack, which serves a small fake help center.
- The widget is one `<script>` tag served from almyty; it has no sign-in, so publishing refuses it on a channel that requires one. See [Publishing a channel](/channels/messaging#website-widget).
- If your documentation has no API, a developer can connect any search endpoint as an HTTP tool: [HTTP tools](/tools/http-tools).
