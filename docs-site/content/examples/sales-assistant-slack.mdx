# Sales assistant in Slack

Give your sales team an assistant in Slack. Before a call, someone asks "What should I know about Kestrel Coffee?" and gets a short brief from your CRM: renewal date and value, open deals, the last conversation and open tickets. After the call, they paste their notes and the assistant saves them to the account, with the next steps.

This guide builds it for Northwind's account managers. It takes about 15 minutes, and there is no code in it. You need someone who can create a Slack app in your Slack workspace for the last step (usually a Slack admin).

**What you need**

- An almyty account with an AI model connected. If you have not done that yet, follow step 1 of the [customer support guide](/examples/customer-support-assistant#1-connect-an-ai-model).
- The description of your CRM's API: a file or link that lists what your CRM can do (often called "OpenAPI" or "Swagger"). An API is the way one piece of software talks to another. Most CRMs publish one; your IT team or the CRM's developer settings have it.
- A Slack workspace where you can create an app.

## 1. Connect your CRM

In the sidebar, open **APIs** and click **Connect an API**. Pick **OpenAPI / Swagger**, then paste the link to your CRM's description on **Link**, or pick **File** and upload it. Click **Connect API**.

almyty works out what your CRM can do. Here it found three things: find accounts by name, get an account with its contacts, deals and recent activity, and add a meeting note to an account. Each becomes a tool the assistant can use.

![The CRM connected, with the three things it can do](/screenshots/guides/sales-1-connect-crm.png)

If your CRM needs a key, click **Add a key** on this page and paste it. The key is stored safely and only ever sent to your CRM.

## 2. Create the assistant

Open **Agents**, click **Create agent** and pick **Autonomous** at the top: you describe the job in plain words and it works out the steps.

- Click the name **New agent** at the top and call it `Sales assistant`.
- **Work mode**, the first section: open **Model** and pick one, for example **gpt-4o**.
- **Personality & style**: `Short and practical. Numbers first, no small talk.`
- **Instructions**:

> You help Northwind account managers. Before a call, look the account up in the CRM and give a short brief: who they are, renewal date and value, open deals, the last conversation and open tickets. After a call, when someone gives you their notes, save them to the account as a meeting note with the next steps, and confirm what you saved.

Then scroll to **Capabilities → Tools and APIs** and tick **All tools** next to your CRM.

![The sales assistant with the CRM's tools ticked](/screenshots/guides/sales-2-agent.png)

Click **Save**, go back to **Agents** with the arrow at the top left, open **Sales assistant** and click **Activate**.

## 3. Ask for a brief before a call

On the **Overview** tab, under **Try it**, type `I have a call with Kestrel Coffee in ten minutes. What should I know?` and press Enter.

![A brief on Kestrel Coffee from the CRM](/screenshots/guides/sales-3-brief.png)

The assistant found the account in the CRM and summed it up: customer since 2022, the $48,000 renewal due on November 30, the open deal, what the COO asked last time, and two open tickets.

## 4. Save notes after the call

Now give it your notes: `Save my notes from the Kestrel call: Dana wants volume pricing for 12 more grinders before the renewal. Next step: I send a quote by Friday.`

![The notes saved to the account in the CRM](/screenshots/guides/sales-4-notes.png)

The assistant added a meeting note to Kestrel Coffee's account in the CRM and told you what it saved. Nobody had to open the CRM.

## 5. Put it in Slack

Your assistant answers in Slack through a Slack app that belongs to you. You create the app once in Slack, and almyty gives you a link that installs it into your workspace.

**In almyty**, open the assistant's **Channels** tab, click **Add channel** and pick **Slack**. Under **Add to Slack**, click **Create one here**.

![The Slack channel asking for your Slack app's details](/screenshots/guides/sales-5-slack-keys.png)

**In Slack**, go to api.slack.com/apps and click **Create New App** (from scratch), name it (for example "Sales assistant") and pick your workspace. On the app's **Basic Information** page you find three values. Copy each into the almyty form:

- **Client ID** (two numbers joined by a dot)
- **Client Secret**
- **Signing Secret** (almyty uses it to check that messages really come from Slack)

**Back in almyty**, click **Save** under the form. The page now shows a **Callback URL**. In your Slack app, open **Event Subscriptions**, turn it on, paste that address as the **Request URL**, and subscribe to the bot events `message.im` (direct messages) and `app_mention` (when someone @mentions it in a channel). Save in Slack.

Then, in almyty, click **Save** at the bottom of the page and **Publish**. You get two addresses under **Install link**:

- a **redirect URL**: add it in your Slack app under **OAuth & Permissions → Redirect URLs**;
- an **install URL**: open it (or send it to your Slack admin) and click **Allow** to add the assistant to your workspace.

![Published: the redirect URL and the install link](/screenshots/guides/sales-6-slack-live.png)

Each workspace that installs it appears under **Workspace installations** on this page, where you can also remove it.

Now anyone on the team can send the assistant a direct message, or @mention it in a channel, and ask for a brief or hand it their notes. In a channel it answers when it is @mentioned, and it knows who asked.

## Good to know

- **It only does what you ticked.** It can read accounts and add notes, because those are the CRM tools you gave it. To keep it read-only, open your CRM under **Capabilities → Tools and APIs**, untick **All tools** and tick only the tools that read.
- **Your CRM key stays in almyty.** Slack users never see it.
- **Who asked what** is kept under the assistant's **Runs** tab, and every change to the assistant is in its **Change history**.
- To have a person check notes before they are saved, add a line to its instructions like "Ask for approval before saving a note", and approve them on **Approvals**. See [Approvals](/approvals).

## For developers

- This walk-through is automated in `scripts/demo-seed/guides.mjs sales` against the local demo stack, which serves a small fake CRM.
- A single workspace can use a bot token instead of the Add to Slack flow: open **Advanced** under **Add to Slack**. See [Publishing a channel](/channels/messaging).
- Installing through the install link asks Slack for these bot permissions: `chat:write`, `app_mentions:read`, `im:history`, `files:read` and `users:read`. In a channel it answers only when it is @mentioned. See [Chat apps](/interfaces) for every platform's setup.
