# Sign in with Microsoft Entra ID, and add and remove people automatically

## What you'll build

almyty behind your company login, set up for Velora Energy (a made-up energy supplier) with Microsoft Entra ID:

- People sign in with their Microsoft work account. There is no separate almyty password to forget.
- When IT adds someone to the almyty app in Entra, they get an almyty account. When IT disables them, they lose access the same day.
- Groups in Entra become teams in almyty.

Eva de Groot started this week. IT added her in Entra; she opened the sign-in link and was in, without an invitation:

![Eva signed in with her Microsoft account on her first day](/screenshots/examples/sso-scim-onboarding/00-signed-in.png)

Single sign-on and automatic provisioning (SCIM) need the **Business** plan. The screenshots come from a test installation: web addresses start with `http://localhost` instead of `https://api.almyty.com`, and Microsoft's sign-in was stood in for by a local service. It takes about 30 minutes, most of it in Entra.

## What you need

- The Business plan, and **owner or admin** in your almyty organization.
- In Microsoft Entra ID, the **Application Administrator** role (or higher).
- Access to your domain's DNS, to add one TXT record.

## 1. Prove you own your email domain

In almyty, open **Settings**, then **People and access → Single sign-on**. Under **Email domains**, enter your domain (`veloraenergy.example` here) and click **Add domain**. The page shows a TXT record:

![The TXT record to add for the domain](/screenshots/examples/sso-scim-onboarding/01-domain-record.png)

Add it at your DNS provider exactly as shown, then click **Check DNS**. Once the record is found the domain shows **Verified**:

![The domain, verified](/screenshots/examples/sso-scim-onboarding/02-domain-verified.png)

almyty only creates accounts, through sign-in or through Entra, for addresses on a verified domain. That stops anyone from creating an account for a mailbox your company does not own. Keep the record in place.

## 2. Register almyty in Entra

On the same almyty page, set **Protocol** to **OpenID Connect**. Under **For your identity provider** you now see two addresses: the **Redirect URI to register** and the **Sign-in link**. Copy the redirect URI.

In the Microsoft Entra admin center (entra.microsoft.com):

1. **Identity → Applications → App registrations → New registration.** **Name**: `almyty`. **Supported account types**: **Accounts in this organizational directory only**. **Redirect URI**: platform **Web**, and paste the redirect URI from almyty. Click **Register**.
2. On the app's **Overview**, copy the **Application (client) ID** and the **Directory (tenant) ID**.
3. **Certificates & secrets → Client secrets → New client secret.** Give it a description and an expiry, click **Add** and copy the **Value** (it is shown once).
4. **Token configuration → Add optional claim → ID → email**, click **Add**, and accept the Microsoft Graph email permission. almyty finds people by their email address, so the sign-in must carry it.

## 3. Connect almyty to Entra

Back in almyty, fill in:

- **Issuer URL**: `https://login.microsoftonline.com/<Directory (tenant) ID>/v2.0`
- **Client ID**: the Application (client) ID
- **Client secret**: the secret's Value
- **Redirect URI**: the same redirect URI you registered in Entra
- Leave **Add people when they first sign in** off: Entra will create the accounts (next step), so nobody gets in who IT has not added.
- Turn on **Use single sign-on**.

![Single sign-on with Entra ID over OpenID Connect](/screenshots/examples/sso-scim-onboarding/03-entra-oidc.png)

Click **Save SSO settings**.

## 4. Let Entra add and remove people

In almyty, under **Automatic member sync (SCIM)**, click **Generate SCIM token**. Copy the token now (it is shown once) and the **SCIM base URL**:

![The SCIM base URL and a new token](/screenshots/examples/sso-scim-onboarding/04-scim-token.png)

In Entra:

1. **Identity → Applications → Enterprise applications**, open **almyty** (the registration made it).
2. **Provisioning → Get started**. **Provisioning Mode**: **Automatic**. Under **Admin Credentials**, **Tenant URL** is the SCIM base URL and **Secret Token** the token. Click **Test Connection**, then **Save**.
3. Under **Settings**, keep **Scope** on **Sync only assigned users and groups**, and set **Provisioning Status** to **On**. Save.
4. **Users and groups → Add user/group**: assign the people and groups who should have almyty. Velora assigns the group `Field service` with Eva de Groot and Tom Hendriks.

Entra then creates Eva and Tom in almyty, creates the team **Field service** and puts them in it. Entra syncs about every 40 minutes; to see it at once, use **Provision on demand** on the **Provisioning** page.

## 5. Hand out the sign-in link

The **Sign-in link** under **For your identity provider** is where people start. Put it in the app's **Properties → Home page URL** in Entra, so almyty shows up in your people's My Apps portal, or share it on your intranet. The normal almyty sign-in page has no single sign-on button.

## Try it

**A new starter.** Eva opens the sign-in link, signs in with her Microsoft account and lands in Velora Energy's almyty (the picture at the top). Her account was made by Entra; she was never invited.

**Who is in.** Entra created Eva and Tom and put both in a new **Field service** team. Then Tom leaves Velora: IT blocks his sign-in in Entra (or removes his assignment), and on its next sync Entra tells almyty he is no longer active. His membership is switched off. **Settings → Members and teams** lists Eva, and Tom no longer appears:

![Members after Entra added Eva and Tom and then disabled Tom](/screenshots/examples/sso-scim-onboarding/05-members.png)

**A leaver.** When Tom opens the sign-in link, Microsoft signs him in but almyty refuses him: "Your access to this organization has been deactivated". (It is shown as a bare error message, not a styled page.) His almyty account itself stays, in case he belongs to another organization.

## Make it yours

- **SAML instead.** Pick **SAML 2.0**: give Entra the **Reply URL** and **Service provider metadata** address shown under **For your identity provider**, and enter Entra's **Login URL**, an entity ID and the signing certificate in almyty.
- **Okta.** The same setup with an Okta OIDC web app (redirect URI under **Sign-in redirect URIs**) and SCIM under the app's **Provisioning** tab.
- **Teams from groups.** Assign more Entra groups to the app; each becomes a team. Then give assistants and keys to those teams (see [Keep each team's assistants to that team](/examples/multi-team-rbac)).

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Check DNS** does not verify | DNS changes take a few minutes. Check the record's name and value are exactly as shown, without quotes added by your DNS provider. |
| Entra's **Test Connection** fails | Paste the SCIM base URL without anything after `/scim/v2`, and the token without spaces. If you clicked **Rotate SCIM token**, give Entra the new one. |
| A person is refused at sign-in with "OIDC claims did not include an email" | Add the **email** optional claim in Entra (step 2.4), and check the person has an email address in Entra. |
| A person is refused although they are in Entra | They are not assigned to the app, Entra has not synced yet, or their address is not on a verified domain. |
| Entra reports a conflict for a group | almyty already has a team with that name, made by hand. Rename or delete that team, or rename the group. |
| An existing almyty user from another company cannot sign in | Single sign-on never adds an existing account to your organization. Invite them instead. |
