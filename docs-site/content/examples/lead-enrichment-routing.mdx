# Score and route new leads

For sales teams where good leads wait in a queue while reps chase the wrong ones. When a new lead comes in, the assistant looks up the company (size, industry), scores it against your ideal customer, writes the score and the reason back into your CRM, and tells the right rep in Slack when it's a hot one.

"CRM" here means your own CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce or any other) connected through its API: almyty has no built-in CRM connector.

For a full walk-through of a sales assistant with a CRM and Slack, see [Sales assistant in Slack](/examples/sales-assistant-slack).

## What you need

- Your CRM's API, and optionally a company-data (enrichment) service with an API.
- A Slack app token that may post messages, for the alerts.
- A way to send new leads to the assistant: your web form or CRM calling it when a lead is created.

## How to build it

1. **Connect your systems.** Open **APIs** and click **Connect an API** for your CRM and your enrichment service. For Slack alerts, create a tool under **Tools**, **Create tool** that calls Slack's "post message" with your app's token from [Credentials](/credentials).
2. **Create the assistant.** Open **Agents**, click **Create agent** and switch to **Autonomous**. In **Instructions**, write your scoring rules: "Look up the company, score it against our ideal customer (mid-sized software companies with 50 or more staff), set an owner, and post hot leads to #sales." Pick the tools under **Tools and APIs**.
3. **Add firm rules.** On its **Constraints** tab: "Never mark a lead with a personal email address as enterprise" and "Always fill in the reason for the score".
4. **Send it leads.** Have your form or CRM call the assistant when a lead is created (see below).

## Ideas

- For borderline cases, set the **Work mode** to **Panel**: two models from different companies argue the fit and a judge decides.
- Draft a first email as part of the run (see [Turn call notes into CRM updates](/examples/meeting-notes-to-crm)).

## For developers

The address to call and sample code are in the **Integration** card on the assistant's **Overview** tab. See the [OpenAI-compatible API](/api-reference/openai-compatible).
