# Cap what your AI assistants spend

## What you'll build

Three limits that keep the AI bill predictable, set up for Larkspur Home, a made-up online homeware shop:

- **The whole organization**: at most $300 a month. Owners and admins get an email at $240 (80%) and again at $300, and runs carry on.
- **One experimental assistant**, `Product copy drafts`: at most $5 a day. At the limit, it stops taking new runs until midnight UTC.
- **Any single run of that assistant**: at most $0.50, so one run that goes round in circles cannot use up the day's budget on its own.

![Two budgets, and the alerts from the one that blocked](/screenshots/examples/govern-ai-spend/00-budgets.png)

Step 6 shows the block happening, with a deliberately tiny limit. The screenshots come from a test installation, so web addresses in them start with `http://localhost`. It takes about 15 minutes.

## What you need

- **Owner or admin** in your almyty organization. Members can see budgets but not change them.
- **At least one agent that has run**, so there is spend to look at. This guide uses Larkspur's assistants from [Check Stripe payouts against Xero](/examples/stripe-payout-reconciliation) and [Ask your data in Slack](/examples/nl-query-your-database), plus `Product copy drafts`, an assistant that writes product descriptions and has no tools.

## 1. See where the money goes

Open **Analytics** in the sidebar and click the **Cost** tab. **Total spend** is this month's (or today's, with **Today**); **Spend by agent** shows how many runs each assistant made and what they cost.

![Analytics, Cost: spend this month and per agent](/screenshots/examples/govern-ai-spend/01-cost.png)

Start from these numbers when you pick limits. Costs are almyty's estimate from the text sent and received and the model maker's price list; the figure on your model maker's invoice is the real one (see [Compare with your model vendor's figures](/cost-governance#compare-with-your-model-vendors-figures)).

## 2. A monthly ceiling for the whole organization

Click the **Budgets** tab, then **New budget**:

1. **Applies to**: **The whole organization**.
2. **Limit (USD)**: `300`. **Period**: **Per month**.
3. **When the limit is reached**: **Warn only**.
4. **Warn at (% of limit)**: `80`.
5. Click **Create budget**.

![The organization budget: $300 a month, warn only](/screenshots/examples/govern-ai-spend/02-org-budget.png)

**Warn only** never stops anything: the support assistant keeps answering customers even in a busy month, and you hear about it at $240 and at $300. Months start on the first, UTC.

## 3. A daily limit for one assistant

Click **New budget** again:

1. **Applies to**: **A single agent**. **Agent**: **Product copy drafts**.
2. **Limit (USD)**: `5`. **Period**: **Per day**.
3. **When the limit is reached**: **Block new runs**. The form says what that means: scheduled runs, chat replies and API calls all stop until the period rolls over.
4. **Warn at (% of limit)**: `80`.
5. Click **Create budget**.

![The agent budget: $5 a day, block new runs](/screenshots/examples/govern-ai-spend/03-agent-budget.png)

Use **Block new runs** for assistants nobody depends on yet. A budget is checked when a run starts, so a run that has started finishes; the next one is refused.

## 4. A cap on each run

A budget adds up many runs. To stop one run from spending too much, set a cap on the assistant itself. Open **Agents**, open **Product copy drafts**, click **Edit**, scroll to **Run limits**, open **Advanced** and set **Spending cap ($)** to `0.50`. Click **Save**.

![Run limits: a spending cap of $0.50 per run](/screenshots/examples/govern-ai-spend/04-run-limit.png)

A run that reaches $0.50 stops there, after the step that crossed it. Without a cap of its own, every run may spend up to $1 (see [Run limits](/run-limits)).

## 5. Try it

Under **Try it** on the agent's **Overview** tab, type:

```
Write product descriptions for: oak serving board (FSC European oak, linseed oil, fits a cheese board for six); linen napkins (set of 4, stonewashed, 45x45 cm, 40 degree wash); ceramic pendant lamp (chalk glaze, 28 cm, 1.5 m cotton cord, E27 up to 60 W).
```

It writes the three descriptions. On the **Budgets** tab, **Period to date** for `Product copy drafts` now shows what that run cost.

## 6. See the block happen

To see a block without spending $5, lower the limit for a minute. On the **Budgets** tab, click the pencil next to **Product copy drafts**, set **Limit (USD)** to `0.02` and click **Save budget**. Then send the same message under **Try it** until the budget is used up. The next one is refused:

![The run refused: the daily budget is used up](/screenshots/examples/govern-ai-spend/05-blocked.png)

Owners and admins get the alert by email and in **Notifications** (the bell at the top right):

![The budget alert in Notifications](/screenshots/examples/govern-ai-spend/06-notification.png)

**Recent budget alerts** on the **Budgets** tab lists every early warning and every limit reached, at most one of each per budget per period. Now set the limit back to `5`.

## Make it yours

- **Several experiments.** Give each experimental assistant its own small daily budget, and keep one monthly budget for the organization.
- **Public assistants.** For an assistant anyone can talk to, also set the spend and message limits per visitor under **Branding and visitor rules** on its **Channels** tab (see [Channels](/channels)).
- **Check the estimate.** Add your OpenAI or Anthropic admin key on the provider's **Settings** tab and click **Sync now** under **Provider actual vs our estimate** on the **Cost** tab, to compare almyty's figures with the invoice.
- **Costs per team.** The Enterprise plan adds a **Chargeback** tab with spend per team and a forecast.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **New budget** is missing or greyed out | Only owners and admins change budgets. Ask one of them. |
| A budget shows **$0.00** although the agent ran | Budgets count agent runs. Chatting with a model outside an agent run is not counted. |
| No alert email arrived | The alert is also in **Notifications**. Each person can turn budget alert emails off for themselves, so check your own notification settings. |
| A run stopped with **BUDGET_EXCEEDED** | That is the per-run cap from step 4, not a budget. Raise **Spending cap ($)** if the work really needs more. |
| Runs are refused after the limit was raised | The refused runs were refused at the time. Send them again; the new limit applies straight away. |
