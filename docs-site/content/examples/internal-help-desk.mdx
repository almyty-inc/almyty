# Internal help desk in Teams or Slack

Give your staff one place to ask everyday questions: "How many vacation days do I have left?", "Can I get a new laptop?", "What is the guest Wi-Fi?". An assistant in Microsoft Teams or Slack answers from your HR and IT systems and from what you tell it, and says where each answer came from. Your HR and IT teams get fewer repeat questions.

This guide builds it for Northwind's staff. It takes about 20 minutes and there is no code in it. Adding it to Teams needs someone who can register an app in your Microsoft account (usually IT).

**What you need**

- An almyty account with an AI model connected. If you have not done that yet, follow step 1 of the [customer support guide](/examples/customer-support-assistant#1-connect-an-ai-model).
- Your intranet or HR system's API description (a file or link listing what the system can do, often called "OpenAPI" or "Swagger"). An API is the way one piece of software talks to another. Northwind's intranet can search the staff handbook, look up leave balances and open IT requests.
- For Teams: an Azure bot registration. For Slack: a Slack app (see the [sales guide](/examples/sales-assistant-slack#5-put-it-in-slack)).

## 1. Connect your intranet

In the sidebar, open **APIs** and click **Connect an API**. Pick **OpenAPI / Swagger**, paste the link to your intranet's description on **Link** (or pick **File** and upload it), and click **Connect API**.

almyty works out what it can do: search the handbook and policies, get an employee's leave balance, and open an IT request.

![The intranet connected, with its three tools](/screenshots/guides/helpdesk-1-connect-intranet.png)

## 2. Add your office guide

Some answers are not in any system: the Wi-Fi, office hours, where to park. If you already have them written down, for example in an office guide, add that text to **Memory** once.

In the sidebar, open **Memory** and click **Add memory**. Under **What should your agents remember?**, paste the whole guide, for example:

> Office guide, Leipzig office.
>
> Wi-Fi: guests use NW-Guest; the password changes every Monday and is on the card at reception. Staff laptops join NW-Staff automatically.
>
> Opening hours: 7:00 to 20:00. After hours, use your badge at the side door.
>
> Parking: the courtyard spaces are for visitors. Staff park in the garage on Lindenstrasse; ask reception for a card.
>
> Kitchen: coffee and tea are free. Please empty the dishwasher if it is clean.

Add **Tags** if you like (`office, wifi`). Open **Advanced** and set **Kind** to **Document**. A document can be as long as you like: for each question, the assistant reads only the parts that answer it. If the guide lives somewhere else too, paste its link into **Where it came from**. Click **Save memory**.

![The office guide, added as a document](/screenshots/guides/helpdesk-2-office-guide.png)

Everything you add on the **Memory** page is read by every assistant whose memory is shared, which is what you set up next.

## 3. Create the assistant

Open **Agents**, click **Create agent** and pick **Autonomous** at the top.

- Click the name **New agent** and call it `Ask HR and IT`.
- **Work mode**, the first section: open **Model** and pick one.
- **Personality & style**: `Friendly and to the point, like a helpful colleague.`
- **Instructions**:

> You answer questions from Northwind staff about HR, IT and the office. Look things up before you answer: the staff handbook and policies, their leave balance (ask for their work email if you need it), and the office guide in memory. Say where the answer came from. If someone needs something done by IT, tell them how to open an IT request. Never share one person's details with another.

Further down, under **Memory**, turn on **Remember between conversations**, set **Whose memory** to **Shared by all agents** (so it reads what you added in step 2) and **What it saves** to **Only when asked** (so it does not fill your shared memory with chat). Under **Capabilities → Tools and APIs**, tick **All tools** next to your intranet.

![Shared memory on, and the intranet's tools ticked](/screenshots/guides/helpdesk-3-agent.png)

Click **Save**, go back to **Agents**, open **Ask HR and IT** and click **Activate**.

## 4. Try it

On the **Overview** tab, under **Try it**, ask: `How many vacation days do I have left? My email is sam.rivera@northwind.example.`

![A leave balance from the HR system, with the handbook section](/screenshots/guides/helpdesk-4-leave.png)

It looked the balance up in the intranet and answered with the numbers and the handbook section. Now ask something only the office guide knows: `What is the guest Wi-Fi at the office?`

![The Wi-Fi answer, taken from the office guide](/screenshots/guides/helpdesk-5-wifi.png)

## 5. Put it in Microsoft Teams

Open the assistant's **Channels** tab, click **Add channel** and pick **Microsoft Teams**. Under **Keys**, click **Create one here**.

![The Teams channel asking for your bot's details](/screenshots/guides/helpdesk-6-teams.png)

**In Azure** (your IT team can do this part): create an **Azure Bot** resource. From it, copy into the almyty form:

- **Microsoft App ID**: the bot's app ID.
- **Client secret**: a secret created for that app.
- **Tenant**: your directory (tenant) ID for a bot only your company uses, or leave `botframework.com` for one other companies can add too.

Click **Save** under the form. Then, in the Azure bot's **Configuration**, set the **Messaging endpoint** to the **Callback URL** almyty shows on the channel page, and turn on the **Microsoft Teams** channel in Azure. Back in almyty, click **Save** at the bottom and **Publish**.

Staff can now find the bot in Teams and message it directly, or add it to a team chat. In a group chat it knows who is asking.

**Prefer Slack?** Add a **Slack** channel instead (or as well). The steps are in the [sales guide](/examples/sales-assistant-slack#5-put-it-in-slack). The same assistant can answer in both.

## Good to know

- **Only staff can reach it.** A Teams or Slack bot answers people in the organizations or workspaces where it is installed. In Teams, entering your own tenant ID keeps it inside your company.
- **Private details stay private.** The instructions tell it never to share one person's details with another. For a firmer line, give it only the tools it needs, and remove any that could look up someone else's records.
- **Keep answers current**: when something changes, delete the old guide on the **Memory** page and add the new one. The next answer uses it.
- **See what people ask**: every question is on the assistant's **Runs** tab. Recurring questions are a good sign that a handbook page needs rewriting.

## For developers

- This walk-through is automated in `scripts/demo-seed/guides.mjs helpdesk` against the local demo stack, which serves a small fake intranet.
- An autonomous agent looks up memories before each answer: facts, and memories you add as **Document** (under **Advanced → Kind**). A document is searched in passages, so the agent gets the parts that relate to the question. For text that changes often or lives in another system, connect that system as an API instead.
