# Keep each team's assistants to that team

## What you'll build

Two teams in one organization that cannot see each other's assistants, connections or keys. It is built for Velora Energy, a made-up energy supplier in Amsterdam:

- **Finance** owns the Stripe connection, the Stripe key and the **Billing assistant**, which can refund customers.
- **Customer support** owns the Zendesk connection and the **Support assistant**.

When Mehmet, who works in customer support, opens **Agents**, he sees his team's assistant and nothing of Finance's:

![Agents as Mehmet in Customer support sees them: only the Support assistant](/screenshots/examples/multi-team-rbac/00-support-view.png)

Teams and the **Who can use it** setting are on every plan. The screenshots come from a test installation, so web addresses in them start with `http://localhost`. It takes about 20 minutes.

## What you need

- **Owner or admin** in your almyty organization. Only they create teams and invite people.
- A connected AI model (see [Connect a provider](/llm-providers)).
- For the Finance side: a Stripe **restricted key**. In Stripe, open **Developers → API keys → Create restricted key**, name it `almyty billing assistant` and set **Customers: Read**, **Charges: Read** and **Refunds: Write**. Leave the rest at **None**.
- For the support side: your Zendesk subdomain (for example `velora.zendesk.com`) and an API token (**Admin Center → Apps and integrations → APIs → Zendesk API → Add API token**). This guide only connects Zendesk; it does not run the support assistant.

## 1. Create the teams

Open **Settings**, then **People and access → Members and teams**. Under **Teams**, click **Create team**, enter the **Team name** and a **Description**, and click **Create team** again. Make two:

- `Finance`: `Billing, refunds and payments. Owns the Stripe connection and the billing assistant.`
- `Customer support`: `Answers customers by email and chat. Owns the Zendesk connection and the support assistant.`

![The two new teams next to Everyone](/screenshots/examples/multi-team-rbac/01-teams.png)

**Everyone** is there from the start and always holds every member. Anything shared with **Everyone** is shared with the whole organization, which is why the next steps pick a team instead.

## 2. Invite your colleagues

On the same page, under **Organization members**, click **Invite member**, enter the **Email address**, pick a **Role** and click **Send invitation**. Velora invites three people:

- `lotte.jansen@veloraenergy.example` as **Admin**. Admins see and manage everything in the organization, so keep this role for the few people who run almyty for everyone.
- `joost.bakker@veloraenergy.example` as **Member**.
- `mehmet.yilmaz@veloraenergy.example` as **Member**.

![Inviting Joost as a member](/screenshots/examples/multi-team-rbac/02-invite.png)

Each of them gets an email and joins once they accept.

## 3. Put people in their team

Under each team, click the **Add a member** icon (the person with a plus), pick the person under **Member** and click **Add member**. Add Joost to **Finance** and Mehmet to **Customer support**. Then set Joost's role in Finance to **Lead** with the menu on his row.

![Joost is the lead of Finance, Mehmet a member of Customer support](/screenshots/examples/multi-team-rbac/03-team-members.png)

A team's **Lead** can approve what that team's assistants ask approval for (see [Decide who signs off](/examples/approval-policies)). A **Member** can use what the team owns.

## 4. Connect Stripe for Finance only

Open **APIs**, click **Connect an API** and pick **OpenAPI / Swagger**. Under **Link to the description**, paste Stripe's own description:

```
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stripe/openapi/master/openapi/spec3.json
```

Open **Advanced**. Next to **Who can use it: Everyone**, click **Change**, pick **One team** and choose **Finance**. Click **Connect API**.

![Connecting Stripe, usable by the Finance team only](/screenshots/examples/multi-team-rbac/04-stripe-finance.png)

almyty reads the description and makes a tool for each of Stripe's 612 operations. On the next screen, add the key: set **How it is sent** to **As a bearer token**, click **Create one here**, name it `Stripe restricted key (billing assistant)` and paste the restricted key from Stripe. Under its **Who can use it**, click **Change** again and pick **One team: Finance**, then **Save**.

![The Stripe key, also for Finance only](/screenshots/examples/multi-team-rbac/04b-stripe-key-finance.png)

The connection and the key are separate things with their own owner. Set both: a Finance-only connection with an organization-wide key would let other teams' tools use the key.

Connect Zendesk the same way for **Customer support**, with this description link:

```
https://developer.zendesk.com/zendesk/oas.yaml
```

Under **Advanced**, set the **Address** to your own subdomain, for example `https://velora.zendesk.com`.

## 5. Give the assistant to the team

Open **Agents**, click **Create agent** and pick **Autonomous**. Name it `Billing assistant`, pick a **Model**, and under **Capabilities → Tools and APIs** tick three Stripe tools: **List all customers**, **List all charges** and **Create a refund**. (The full instructions are in [Decide who signs off](/examples/approval-policies), which builds on this assistant.)

At the top, the button next to **Draft** says who can use it, **Everyone** for now. Click it, pick **One team** and choose **Finance**. Click **Save**, then **Activate** on the assistant's page.

![The billing assistant: only members of Finance can see and use it](/screenshots/examples/multi-team-rbac/05-agent-team.png)

Create the `Support assistant` the same way with two Zendesk tools, **List Tickets** and **Show Ticket**, for **One team: Customer support**.

## Try it

Sign in as Mehmet. **Agents** shows the **Support assistant** with the tag **team: Customer support**, and no **Billing assistant** (the screenshot at the top). **APIs** shows Zendesk and not Stripe, and **Credentials** does not list the Stripe key.

A link to the billing assistant does not help either. Opened by Mehmet, it says the agent does not exist:

![Mehmet opening the billing assistant's link: Agent not found](/screenshots/examples/multi-team-rbac/06-support-denied.png)

Joost, in Finance, sees the billing assistant and can run it. Lotte, as an admin, sees both teams' assistants.

## Make it yours

- **More teams.** One team per group that owns its own systems: IT with Jira and Okta, Sales with HubSpot. A person can be in several teams.
- **Shared basics.** Keep things everyone needs, such as the AI model connection, on **Everyone**.
- **Only you.** For a personal experiment, pick **Only you**: not even admins see it.
- **Company sign-in.** With [single sign-on](/examples/sso-scim-onboarding), your directory adds and removes people for you, and its groups become teams.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| A team member cannot see the assistant | Check the button next to **Draft** on the assistant: it must name their team. Being in a team with the same name in your directory is not enough until that group is synced (see [Company sign-in](/examples/sso-scim-onboarding)). |
| The assistant cannot call Stripe for a team member | The key must be usable by that team too. Open **Credentials**, then the key, and check **Who can use it**. |
| The assistant's **Access** card says it has not been given the Stripe key | That is about the assistant answering on a channel (web chat, Slack) on its own behalf: it cannot use a team's key there. Runs started by a team member, like **Try it**, use the member's own access. |
