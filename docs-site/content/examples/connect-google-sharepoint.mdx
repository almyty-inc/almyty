# Answer sales questions from Google Drive

## What you'll build

An assistant that answers your sales team's questions from the documents in your shared Google Drive: pricing rules, discount limits, security answers, current proposals. An account executive asks "Can I give Kaffeewerk 15% off an annual Business plan with 8 seats?". The assistant searches the drive, reads the pricing document and the Kaffeewerk proposal, answers with the rule that applies, and links both documents. It only reads: it never creates, changes or shares a file.

![The answer: not without approval, the discount rule from the pricing document, and links to both documents](/screenshots/examples/connect-google-sharepoint/00-answer.png)

This guide builds it for Parcelwise, a made-up company that sells shipping software to online shops, with made-up documents. The screenshots come from a test installation, so web addresses in them start with `http://localhost`. If your documents are in SharePoint or OneDrive, see [SharePoint and OneDrive instead](#sharepoint-and-onedrive-instead).

## What you need

- **An almyty account** with an AI model connected (**Models**, **Connect a provider**; see step 1 of [Answer product questions from your help center](/examples/docs-deflection-bot#1-connect-an-ai-model)).
- **Your documents in a Google shared drive**, here one called "Sales". Open it in Google Drive: the last part of the address, after `/folders/`, is its ID (here `0APwSalesShared`).
- **About 30 minutes in Google Cloud**, once, to register an app that may read Drive. Ask your IT person if your company manages Google Workspace.

## 1. Register a Google app for Drive

Google lets an app read Drive only when the app is registered with Google. You register your own; almyty signs in through it. This happens in Google's console, so there are no almyty screenshots for it. [Connect Google](/credentials/connect-google) has every step in detail.

1. Open **console.cloud.google.com**, create a project (for example `almyty sales documents`).
2. Under **APIs & Services**, **Library**, turn on the **Google Drive API**.
3. Set up the **OAuth consent screen**. With Google Workspace, choose **Internal**: only people in your company can use it and Google does not need to review it.
4. Under **Clients** (or **Credentials**, **Create credentials**, **OAuth client ID**), create a client of type **Web application**. Leave **Authorized redirect URIs** empty for now.
5. Keep the **Client ID** and **Client secret**.

## 2. Connect Google Drive

In almyty, open **APIs**, click **Connect an API**, pick **OpenAPI / Swagger** and paste Google's own description of the Drive API under **Link to the description**:

```
https://www.googleapis.com/discovery/v1/apis/drive/v3/rest
```

![The link to Google Drive's description, pasted](/screenshots/examples/connect-google-sharepoint/01-drive-link.png)

Open **Advanced**, set **Name** to `Google Drive` (so the tool names match the ones below) and click **Connect API**. almyty makes a tool for everything the Drive API can do: 64 of them. Drive signs in with Google, and almyty has already picked that.

![Finish connecting Google Drive: 64 tools, and a Google sign-in needed](/screenshots/examples/connect-google-sharepoint/02-drive-setup.png)

## 3. Sign in with Google, read-only

Under **Sign-in**, click **Create one here**.

1. Copy the redirect address almyty shows (it ends in `/credentials/oauth2/callback`). **In Google Cloud**, add it to your client under **Authorized redirect URIs** and save.
2. Back in almyty, paste the **Client ID** and **Client secret**.
3. Under **What it asks for**, untick everything except `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly`. With only this permission, even a mistake cannot change or delete a file.
4. Click **Sign in**, pick your Google account and allow access.

![The Google sign-in form: redirect address, client ID and secret, and only drive.readonly ticked](/screenshots/examples/connect-google-sharepoint/03-google-sign-in.png)

The page then says **Saved. Tools send it with every call.**

![Google Drive connected and signed in](/screenshots/examples/connect-google-sharepoint/04-signed-in.png)

## 4. Check that it can find your documents

Before building the assistant, try the search tool once. Open **Tools**, search for `drive_files_list` and open **google_drive_drive_files_list**. On **Test tool**, put `fullText contains 'discount'` under **q** and click **Execute Tool**.

![The Drive search tool, tested: the files that mention "discount"](/screenshots/examples/connect-google-sharepoint/05-test-tool.png)

**Success** and a list of files means the sign-in works and the documents are readable. Use one search term per query here: almyty's security check blocks queries that join conditions with `and` after a quoted word, such as `fullText contains 'discount' and trashed = false`, as a possible injection. That is why the instructions below limit the search to the shared drive with its own fields instead.

## 5. Create the assistant

Open **Agents**, click **Create agent**, pick **Autonomous**, name it `Sales documents` and pick a model under **Work mode** (for example **gpt-4o**).

- **Personality & style**: `Direct and exact. Gives the answer first, then the rule it comes from.`
- **Instructions** (put your shared drive's ID in place of `0APwSalesShared`):

```
You answer Parcelwise sales questions from the documents in the team's Google Drive: pricing, discounts, security, proposals and onboarding.

For every question:
1. Search the "Sales" shared drive for the documents that cover it: search the full text (q: fullText contains 'word'), newest first, with corpora "drive", driveId "0APwSalesShared", and includeItemsFromAllDrives and supportsAllDrives set to true.
2. Read the one or two most relevant documents as plain text.
3. Answer in a few lines, quoting the rule or number the document gives, and end with "Sources:" and a link to each document you used.

If no document covers the question, say so. Never guess a price, a discount or a security claim. You only read: never create, change or share files.
```

![The Sales documents assistant with its instructions](/screenshots/examples/connect-google-sharepoint/06-agent.png)

## 6. Give it two Drive tools

Under **Capabilities**, **Tools and APIs**, search for and tick:

| Tool | Drive endpoint | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| `google_drive_drive_files_list` | `GET /drive/v3/files` | Search the shared drive |
| `google_drive_drive_files_export` | `GET /drive/v3/files/{fileId}/export` | Read a Google Doc as plain text (`mimeType: text/plain`) |

![The two Drive tools picked](/screenshots/examples/connect-google-sharepoint/07-tools.png)

Click **Save**, go back to the agent and click **Activate**.

## 7. Try it

Under **Try it** on the **Overview** tab, type:

```
Can I give Kaffeewerk 15% off an annual Business plan with 8 seats?
```

The assistant searched the shared drive for "discount" and for "Kaffeewerk", read the two documents it found, and answered:

> **Not on your own.** 8 seats is below the 10-seat minimum for an extra discount, so it needs approval from Sofia Marques.
>
> - The pricing document says account executives may give up to 10% extra on annual Growth or Business plans with 10 or more seats; anything above that needs Sofia Marques.
> - Annual plans already get 15% off the monthly price, before any extra discount.
> - The current draft, "Kaffeewerk - proposal draft", proposes 10% for 8 seats.
>
> Sources: Kaffeewerk - proposal draft, Pricing and discounts 2026

Each source is a link that opens the Google Doc. The **Runs** tab shows the searches and the documents it read.

## Make it yours

- **Ask it in Slack.** Add a **Slack** channel on the **Channels** tab (steps in [Answer product questions from your help center](/examples/docs-deflection-bot#7-put-it-in-slack)). On the **Overview** tab, under **Access**, let the agent use your Google sign-in, because a Slack message is not you. Everyone who can write to the bot can then read what your sign-in can read, so sign in with an account that sees only the sales drive.
- **Files other than Google Docs.** Export works only for Google Docs, Sheets and Slides. For uploaded files, also tick `google_drive_drive_files_get` and ask it to fetch the file with `alt` set to `media`; plain text and CSV files read well this way, PDFs and Word files less so.

## SharePoint and OneDrive instead

Microsoft publishes the OpenAPI descriptions of Microsoft Graph, the API behind SharePoint and OneDrive, split by area. The search area connects like Drive in step 2:

- **Link to the description**: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/microsoftgraph/msgraph-sdk-powershell/main/openApiDocs/v1.0/Search.yml`
- **Name**: `Microsoft Search`. almyty makes 63 tools; the one you need is `microsoft_search_search_query` (`POST /search/query`), which searches SharePoint and OneDrive at once.

Sign in with an app you register in **Microsoft Entra** (**App registrations**, **New registration**, a **Web** redirect URI copied from almyty), with the delegated permissions `Files.Read.All` and `Sites.Read.All`. Tick only `microsoft_search_search_query`, and in the instructions ask it to search with `entityTypes` `["driveItem"]` and to answer from each hit's summary, linking its `webUrl`.

Two limits today: each search hit carries a short summary of the matching text, not the whole document; and Microsoft's description of the files area, which has the "read a file" operation, is too large to connect (over 1,000 operations, more than almyty takes from one description). This variant was connected on a test installation but not walked end to end like the Google one above.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Security check blocked tool "google_drive_drive_files_list": SQL injection in input "q"** | The query joins conditions with `and` after a quoted word. Search for one term per call and limit the drive with `corpora` and `driveId` instead (step 4). |
| Google says **Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch** | Add almyty's redirect address to your Google client under **Authorized redirect URIs** (step 3). |
| **Request had insufficient authentication scopes** | The sign-in lacks `drive.readonly`. Sign in again on the Google Drive API page and tick it. |
| The search finds nothing in the shared drive | Check the drive ID in the instructions, and that the Google account you signed in with is a member of that shared drive. |
| In Slack it answers **I don't have access to Google Drive** | On the **Overview** tab, under **Access**, let the agent use your Google sign-in. |
