# Summarise on-call alerts

For on-call engineers woken by a wall of alerts with no context. When an alert fires, the assistant gathers the related metrics and recent logs, compares them with past incidents, and posts a plain-language summary with the most likely runbook to your incident channel. The person on call starts fixing instead of gathering.

## What you need

- Your monitoring and logging tools' APIs (for example Datadog, PagerDuty or Grafana Loki), and your runbooks somewhere searchable.
- A Slack app token that may post messages, for the summary.
- Your alerting tool able to call a web address when an alert fires.

## How to build it

1. **Connect your signals.** Open **APIs** and click **Connect an API** for your monitoring and logging tools, and for your runbook search. For posting the summary, create a tool under **Tools**, **Create tool** that calls Slack's "post message" with your app's token from [Credentials](/credentials).
2. **Create the assistant.** Open **Agents**, click **Create agent** and switch to **Autonomous**. In **Instructions**, write: "Given an alert, gather the related metrics and the last 15 minutes of logs, compare with earlier incidents, and post a short summary with the likely cause and runbook to #incidents." Pick the tools under **Tools and APIs**.
3. **Let it remember.** Turn on **Remember between conversations** with **This agent's own** memory, so a recurring incident comes with last time's fix attached.
4. **Keep it advisory.** It summarises and suggests; give it no tools that change your systems. If you later want it to act, have it ask for approval first (see [Approvals](/approvals)).
5. **Trigger it.** Point your alerting tool's webhook at the assistant (see below).

## Ideas

- Let it run a read-only check on the affected machine through a runner: see [Let an assistant work on one of your machines](/examples/expose-any-machine).
- Add your post-incident reviews to memory, so the summaries get better over time.

## For developers

The address to call and sample code are in the **Integration** card on the assistant's **Overview** tab. If your alerting tool cannot send what the endpoint expects, a small relay can sit in between. See the [OpenAI-compatible API](/api-reference/openai-compatible).
