# What you can do

almyty turns the software your company already runs into AI assistants that do real work, and puts them where your staff and customers already talk. Here is what that looks like in practice.

The six step-by-step guides below build a complete assistant each, with a screenshot for every step. They are written for anyone: you do not need to write code. The shorter examples after them show more ideas, in fewer words.

![Assistants built from these guides, on the Agents page](/screenshots/agents-list.png)

## Step-by-step guides

- **[Customer support assistant](/examples/customer-support-assistant)**: one assistant on your website, WhatsApp and email. It looks up orders in your order system, refunds small amounts itself and asks a manager before a big refund.
- **[Sales assistant in Slack](/examples/sales-assistant-slack)**: before a call, a short brief on the account from your CRM; after the call, your notes saved back to it.
- **[Product chat for your website](/examples/website-product-chat)**: a chat on your website and campaign pages that answers product questions from your documentation, with a daily spending cap.
- **[Nightly systems report](/examples/nightly-systems-report)**: an assistant that checks your systems every night and posts a short report to Slack.
- **[Internal help desk](/examples/internal-help-desk)**: an assistant in Microsoft Teams or Slack that answers staff questions about HR, IT and the office.
- **[For developers](/examples/developers-apis-and-machines)**: your company's APIs as tools in coding assistants, and agents that run jobs on your own machines.

## How every guide works

Each assistant is built from the same few pieces:

1. **Connect your systems.** Under **APIs**, give almyty the description of a system you already use (an order system, a CRM, a help center). It works out what that system can do, such as "look up an order" or "issue a refund", and turns each into a tool an assistant can use.
2. **Build the assistant.** Under **Agents**, write what it should do in plain words, pick the AI model it runs on (you can switch any time) and tick the tools it may use.
3. **Put it where people are.** On its **Channels** tab, publish it as a web chat, a chat bubble on your website, in Slack, Teams, WhatsApp, email, text messages, iMessage, as a desktop or terminal app, or for other AI tools.

Around all of it: your keys are stored safely under **Credentials**, you decide who may use what, spending caps and visitor limits keep costs in check, anything that cannot be undone can wait for a person's approval, and every action is recorded.
