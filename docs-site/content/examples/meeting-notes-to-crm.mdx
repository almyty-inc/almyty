# Turn call notes into CRM updates

For reps who have good calls and then never update the CRM. Paste your notes (or a transcript) after a call. The assistant pulls out the next steps, updates the deal in your CRM, and drafts a follow-up email. Anything that leaves the company waits for your approval first.

"CRM" here means your own CRM connected through its API: almyty has no built-in CRM connector.

For a full walk-through with screenshots, see [Sales assistant in Slack](/examples/sales-assistant-slack): it looks up an account before a call and writes the meeting notes back afterwards.

## What you need

- Your CRM's API (and, if it should send the email itself, your email service's API).

## How to build it

1. **Connect the CRM.** Open **APIs**, click **Connect an API**, and connect your CRM. Its operations, such as "update deal" and "log activity", become tools.
2. **Create the assistant.** Open **Agents**, click **Create agent** and switch to **Autonomous**. In **Instructions**, write: "From these notes, list the action items, update the deal stage and next-step date, and draft a follow-up email in the rep's voice." Pick the CRM tools under **Tools and APIs**.
3. **Keep the CRM honest.** On its **Constraints** tab, add "Never move a deal to a stage the notes don't support" and "Never invent a commitment".
4. **Approve before anything goes out.** Add to its instructions: "Ask for approval before sending any email." When it asks, the draft and the reason appear on the **Approvals** page; you approve or reject it.
5. **Use it.** Paste the notes into its web chat or Slack (add the channel on its **Channels** tab), or have your note-taking tool send the notes to it when a call ends.

## Ideas

- Only ask for approval on the outgoing email, and let the CRM update go through on its own.
- Turn on memory so it remembers each account's tone and earlier promises.
