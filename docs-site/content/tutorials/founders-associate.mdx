# Tutorial: a founder's associate

## What you'll build

An assistant that looks after your day. Every weekday morning it reads all your calendars (work, team and personal), your inbox and your task list, and emails you a short brief: meetings that clash, today's meetings in order, tasks that are due or late, your top three priorities, and a prep note for every meeting with someone from outside the company: who they are, how you know them, what you last wrote to each other and what is still open. It remembers what it learns about people, so "how do we know each other" gets better over time. It only reads: it never sends email, answers invitations or changes your tasks.

![The morning brief as it arrives by email: two clashing meetings, the day in order, two tasks, three priorities and prep notes](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/00-morning-brief.png)

The screenshots in this tutorial come from a test installation with made-up people (Maya Chen, founder of "Lumen"), so web addresses in them start with `http://localhost`. Yours start with your almyty address.

## What you need

- **An almyty account**, and the key of an AI provider, such as OpenAI.
- **A Google account** with the calendars, mail and tasks the assistant should read. A Google Workspace account (your company's Google) is easiest, as you will see in step 2.
- **About 30 minutes in Google Cloud**, once, to let almyty sign in to your Google account. Google asks every app that reads someone's calendar or mail to be registered there first. Ask your IT person if your company manages Google Workspace.
- **An email sending service account with Resend** (resend.com), with your company's domain set up, for the morning email. If you would rather get the brief in Slack, see [Make it yours](#make-it-yours).
- **Time:** about an hour in total.

## 1. Connect an AI model

In the sidebar, open **Models** and click **Connect a provider**.

1. **Name:** `OpenAI`.
2. **Provider:** start typing `OpenAI` and pick it.
3. **API key:** paste the key from your OpenAI account (platform.openai.com, API keys; **Get a key** next to the field opens that page).
4. Click **Save**.

![Connect a provider, with OpenAI picked and the key pasted](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/01-connect-openai.png)

almyty checks the key with OpenAI and lists the models you can use. **Key works** means you are done.

![OpenAI connected: Key works, and its models with their prices](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/02-openai-connected.png)

## 2. Register almyty with Google (once)

This step happens in Google's own console, not in almyty, so there are no almyty screenshots for it. You do it once; all three Google services use it.

1. Open **console.cloud.google.com** and sign in with the Google account the assistant should read. Create a project, for example "almyty assistant".
2. Under **APIs & Services → Library**, turn on three APIs: **Google Calendar API**, **Gmail API** and **Google Tasks API**.
3. Set up the **OAuth consent screen** (Google also calls it the **Google Auth Platform**): an app name such as "Lumen assistant" and your email.
   - With **Google Workspace**, choose **Internal**. Only people in your company can use it, and Google does not need to review it.
   - With a personal Google account, choose **External**, leave it in **Testing**, and add your own address under **Test users**. Google ends such sign-ins after 7 days, so you sign in again once a week. Publishing the app for good needs Google's review, because reading mail is something Google checks closely.
4. Under **Clients** (or **Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID**), create a client of type **Web application**. Leave **Authorized redirect URIs** open for now: you copy the address in from almyty in the next step.
5. Keep the **Client ID** and **Client secret** that Google shows you.

## 3. Connect Google Calendar

1. Open **APIs** and click **Connect an API**.

![Connect an API: pick what kind of API it is](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/03-connect-an-api.png)

2. Pick **OpenAPI / Swagger**. Under **Link to the description**, paste:

```
https://api.apis.guru/v2/specs/googleapis.com/calendar/v3/openapi.yaml
```

![The link to Google Calendar's description, pasted](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/04-calendar-link.png)

   This is a description of Google Calendar's API in the format almyty reads. (Google's own description, the "discovery document", is in a different format that almyty does not read yet.) Optionally open **Advanced** and set **Name** to `Google Calendar`.

3. Click **Connect API**. almyty reads the description and makes a tool for everything Google Calendar can do: 37 of them.

![Finish connecting Google Calendar: 37 tools made, and a Google sign-in needed](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/05-finish-connecting.png)

4. Under **Sign-in**, click **Create one here**. The form shows a **redirect address**. Copy it with the button next to it, go back to your Google client from step 2, add it under **Authorized redirect URIs** and save there.
5. Back in almyty, paste the **Client ID** and **Client secret** from Google and click **Sign in**.

![The Google sign-in form: the redirect address to copy, the client ID and the client secret](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/06-google-sign-in.png)

6. Google opens its consent screen. It names your app and lists what almyty asks for. For Google Calendar that is broad: it lists seeing and editing your calendars, because almyty asks for everything the description offers. Your assistant only gets the reading tools you tick in step 6. Click **Continue** (or **Allow**).
7. You come back to almyty: **Saved. Tools send it with every call.** The sign-in is kept under **Credentials** and renews itself.

![Google Calendar signed in](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/07-calendar-signed-in.png)

## 4. Connect Gmail and Google Tasks

Do step 3 twice more, with these links:

| Service | Link to paste |
|---|---|
| Gmail | `https://api.apis.guru/v2/specs/googleapis.com/gmail/v1/openapi.yaml` |
| Google Tasks | `https://api.apis.guru/v2/specs/googleapis.com/tasks/v1/openapi.yaml` |

For each, click **Create one here** and **Sign in** again with the same client ID and secret. Each Google service needs its own sign-in: the list offers your Calendar sign-in too, but it only covers the calendar, so Gmail would refuse it. You do not need to add a new redirect address; it is the same one.

**APIs** now lists all three.

![The three Google APIs: Gmail with 79 tools, Google Calendar with 37, Google Tasks with 14](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/08-apis.png)

## 5. Check that the tools work

Open **Tools** and search for `calendar_events_list`.

![Searching the tools for calendar_events_list](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/09-tools-search.png)

Open **google_calendar_calendar_events_list**, go to **Test tool**, type `primary` into **calendarId** and click **Execute Tool**. You should see your own calendar entries. If you get an error, see [If something goes wrong](#if-something-goes-wrong).

![The tool test: Success, with this week's events from the calendar](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/10-test-tool.png)

## 6. Create the assistant

Open **Agents**, click **Create agent** and pick **Autonomous** at the top.

1. Click the name **New agent** and call it `Founder's associate`.
2. **Work mode → Model:** pick **gpt-4o** (or another strong model you connected).
3. **Personality & style:**

```
Calm, brief and specific, like a trusted chief of staff. Facts first, no filler. If something is unclear or missing, say so instead of guessing.
```

4. **Instructions** (change the names to yours):

```
You are Maya's associate at Lumen.

Every morning, write Maya a short brief for the day:
1. Conflicts first: any meetings that overlap, across all her calendars (work, team and personal), with a suggestion for which one to move.
2. Today's meetings from all her calendars, in time order.
3. Open tasks that are due today or overdue.
4. The top three priorities for the day, based on the meetings, the tasks and recent emails.

Before each meeting with someone from outside the company, add a short prep note: who they are, how Maya knows them (check what you remember and past emails), what was discussed last time, and the open points.

Remember new facts about people: who introduced whom, where they work, what they care about.

Only read calendars, emails and tasks. Never send email, answer invitations or change tasks.
```

![The assistant's model, personality and instructions](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/11-agent-instructions.png)

5. **Memory:** turn on **Remember between conversations**. Set **Whose memory** to **This agent's own** and **What it saves** to **Facts it learns**.

![Memory: its own, saving the facts it learns](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/12-agent-memory.png)

6. **Capabilities → Tools and APIs:** search for and tick these six tools. They only read:

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `google_calendar_calendar_calendar_list_list` | lists your calendars |
| `google_calendar_calendar_events_list` | reads a calendar's events |
| `gmail_gmail_users_messages_list` | finds emails |
| `gmail_gmail_users_messages_get` | reads one email |
| `google_tasks_tasks_tasklists_list` | lists your task lists |
| `google_tasks_tasks_tasks_list` | reads the tasks in a list |

![The six reading tools ticked](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/13-agent-tools.png)

7. Click **Save**, go back with the arrow at the top left and click **Activate**.

## 7. Try it

On the assistant's **Overview** tab, under **Try it**, type `Write my morning brief for today.` and press Enter.

![The first brief in Try it: two clashes found across the work, team and personal calendars](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/14-first-run.png)

It found both clashes, even though the dentist appointment is on the personal calendar and the hiring sync on the team calendar. Try it shows the text as written, so headings appear between `**` marks.

To see how it got there, open the **Runs** tab and click the run. Each step shows which tool it called, with what, and what came back.

![The run step by step: memory, calendars, tasks, emails](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/15-run-steps.png)

## 8. Get the brief by email

The brief goes out through an **Email** channel. You need a Resend account with your domain verified (resend.com → Domains) and an API key (resend.com → API Keys).

1. Open the assistant's **Channels** tab, click **Add channel** and pick **Email**.

![Add channel: pick Email](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/16-add-channel.png)

2. Under **Credential**, click **Create one here**. Name it, paste the Resend **API key**, then open **More options** and fill in:
   - **From address:** the address the brief comes from, on your verified domain, such as `associate@lumen.example`.
   - **Inbound address:** an address on a domain whose mail Resend receives for you, such as `associate@in.lumen.example`. The form calls it optional, but the channel cannot be published without it.
3. Click **Save**, then **Save** at the bottom of the page, then **Publish**.

![The Resend key, From address and Inbound address](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/17-email-key.png)

The channel shows **Live**.

![The Email channel, live](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/18-email-live.png)

## 9. Run it every weekday morning

On the **Overview** tab, under **Schedule**, click **Set up a schedule**.

1. **Repeat:** **At a time of day**. **Days:** **Weekdays (Monday to Friday)**. **Time:** `07:00`. **Time zone:** yours.
2. **Send the result to:** **Email**, with your own address.
3. **Message:**

```
Write my morning brief for today: conflicts first, then meetings, tasks due, top three priorities, and a prep note for every meeting with someone from outside the company.
```

4. Click **Save schedule**.

![Set up a schedule: weekdays at 7:00, the result emailed](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/19-schedule.png)

The **Schedule** card on the Overview tab now says when it runs next and where the result goes.

![The schedule card: every weekday at 7:00, next run, posts to your email](/screenshots/tutorials/founder/20-schedule-card.png)

## 10. The next morning

At 7:00 the brief arrives in your inbox, like the one at the top of this page. Every run is kept under **Runs**, with its cost.

The **Memory** tab shows what it has learned about people, for example that Tom Becker introduced you to Priya Raman and that you worked with Tom before. It uses this in later prep notes. You can delete anything here.

## Make it yours

- **Outlook and Microsoft 365 instead of Google.** Microsoft publishes an OpenAPI description of Microsoft Graph, which covers Outlook mail, calendars and To Do tasks. It is very large: connect it under **APIs** the same way, register an app in Microsoft Entra (the Microsoft counterpart of step 2), and tick only the calendar, mail-reading and task-reading tools. Each Microsoft sign-in works the same way as Google's in step 3.
- **Another task app.** Any task app that publishes an OpenAPI description works: connect it under **APIs**, add its key, and tick its "list tasks" tool instead of Google Tasks.
- **The brief in Slack instead of email.** Add a **Slack** channel instead of Email (see the [biz-dev tutorial](/tutorials/biz-dev-assistant#3-put-it-in-slack)) and pick it under **Send the result to**.
- **A shorter or longer brief.** Change the instructions in plain words: "skip the priorities on Fridays", "only flag clashes with external meetings".
- **Prep notes during the day.** **Always on** can wake the assistant every 30 minutes to prepare the next meeting. Today it reports either after every wake (including "nothing to prepare") or only when it changed something, which a read-only assistant never does, so for now the morning brief with all of the day's prep notes works better.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **This link doesn't return an API description.** | You pasted Google's own discovery document. Use the links in this tutorial. |
| Google says **Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch** | The redirect address from step 3 is not on your Google client. Copy it again from the sign-in form and add it under **Authorized redirect URIs**. |
| Google says **access blocked** or **app not verified** | With a personal account, add yourself under **Test users** on the consent screen (step 2). |
| Gmail calls fail with **insufficient permission** or **403** | You picked the Calendar sign-in for Gmail. Open Gmail under **APIs**, and under **Key** sign in again with **Create one here**. |
| A week later, every call fails | Your Google app is **External** and in **Testing**: Google ends those sign-ins after 7 days. Sign in again, or use a Workspace **Internal** app. |
| The team or personal calendar comes back empty, or a search finds nothing, and the run shows addresses like `*****mple` | Your organization's compliance policy masks email addresses in what tools send. An admin can untick **Email addresses** under **Settings → Advanced → Compliance**. |
| Publishing the Email channel says **The Email credential still needs: inbound address** | Open the Resend credential on **Credentials**, **Replace key**, fill in **Inbound address** under **More options**, then pick the credential again on the channel. |
| Connecting a model you run yourself (Ollama) says **Could not reach almyty to check the key** | The model took longer than 30 seconds to load. Start it once (`ollama run <model>`) and try again. |
| Always on says it **was paused because the agent woke more often in an hour than it may** | **Wake now** counts too. Wait an hour, or click **Turn it back on**. |
