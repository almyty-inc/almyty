# Tutorials

Each tutorial builds one complete assistant from an empty account, one click at a time, with a screenshot for every step and the text to paste. They are written for anyone: you do not need to write code. Where a step happens outside almyty (Google's console, HubSpot, Slack), the tutorial says exactly what to do there.

- **[A founder's associate](/tutorials/founders-associate)**: every weekday morning, a brief by email from all your calendars, your inbox and your tasks: clashing meetings, the day in order, what is due, your top three priorities and a prep note for every outside meeting. It remembers how you know people. Uses Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Tasks.
- **[A business development assistant](/tutorials/biz-dev-assistant)**: finds prospects that match your target profile in your CRM, writes each decision maker a personal email that waits for your approval, adds the deals to your pipeline, and follows up after three days without a reply. Uses HubSpot, Gmail and Slack.
- **[New customer onboarding](/tutorials/customer-onboarding)**: an agent that works for weeks. When a deal is won it shares a folder with the new customer and sends a welcome email, waits for their files, sends a friendly reminder after three days, asks you to approve the kickoff call, then hands over to your colleague and records every step on the deal. Between its checks nothing runs, so it survives restarts. Uses HubSpot, Google Drive, Gmail and Google Calendar.

Shorter guides for other jobs are under [What you can do](/examples).
