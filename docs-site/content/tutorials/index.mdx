# Tutorials

Each tutorial builds one complete assistant from an empty account, one click at a time, with a screenshot for every step and the text to paste. They are written for anyone: you do not need to write code. Where a step happens outside almyty (Google's console, HubSpot, Slack, Telegram), the tutorial says exactly what to do there.

- **[A founder's associate](/tutorials/founders-associate)**: every weekday morning, a brief by email from all your calendars, your inbox and your tasks: clashing meetings, the day in order, what is due, your top three priorities and a prep note for every outside meeting. It remembers how you know people. Uses Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Tasks.
- **[A business development assistant](/tutorials/biz-dev-assistant)**: finds prospects that match your target profile in your CRM, writes each decision maker a personal email that waits for your approval, adds the deals to your pipeline, and follows up after three days without a reply. Uses HubSpot, Gmail and Slack.
- **[A personal assistant that gets to know you](/tutorials/personal-assistant)**: you talk to it in Telegram, and it remembers what you tell it: your routines, the people in your life, what you said yes to. Every 30 minutes it looks at your calendar, mail and tasks, tells you when something needs you (a moved flight that clashes with a meeting), offers a fix, and changes nothing until you approve. Uses Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Tasks and Telegram.

Shorter guides for other jobs are under [What you can do](/examples).
