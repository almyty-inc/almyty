# Tutorial: a business development assistant

## What you'll build

An assistant that does your first round of outreach. You tell it in Slack "find new prospects and reach out". It looks in your CRM for companies that match your target profile, finds the right person at each (the COO, a VP or the head of operations), skips anyone you are already talking to, and writes each one a short, personal email. Nothing goes out until you approve it: each email waits on the **Approvals** page, where you read exactly who it goes to and what it says. Once sent, it adds a deal to your CRM pipeline and writes a LinkedIn message for you to send yourself (it never touches LinkedIn). Every few hours it checks for replies: a reply moves the deal forward and you get told what they said; no reply after three days gets one friendly follow-up in the same email thread, again only after you approve it.

![An outreach email waiting for approval: who it goes to, the subject and the full text](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/00-approve-email.png)

The screenshots in this tutorial come from a test installation with made-up people and companies, so web addresses in them start with `http://localhost`. Yours start with your almyty address.

## What you need

- **An almyty account** with an AI model connected (step 1 of the [founder's associate tutorial](/tutorials/founders-associate#1-connect-an-ai-model)).
- **Gmail connected**, which the assistant sends from: steps 2 to 4 of the [founder's associate tutorial](/tutorials/founders-associate#2-register-almyty-with-google-once) (only the Gmail part).
- **A HubSpot account** with your target companies and their contacts in it, and permission to create a private app (usually a HubSpot admin).
- **A Slack workspace** where you can create an app (usually a Slack admin).
- **Time:** about an hour.

## 1. Connect HubSpot

HubSpot describes its CRM in several parts. You need three: companies, contacts and deals.

1. Open **APIs**, click **Connect an API** and pick **OpenAPI / Swagger**.
2. Under **Link to the description**, paste the companies link:

```
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HubSpot/HubSpot-public-api-spec-collection/main/PublicApiSpecs/CRM/Companies/Rollouts/424/v3/companies.json
```

![The link to HubSpot's companies description, pasted](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/01-hubspot-link.png)

3. Open **Advanced**, set **Name** to `HubSpot Companies` and click **Connect API**.
4. On **Finish connecting**, the key is set to **Sent as the hapikey query parameter**. That is HubSpot's old developer key, which no longer reads CRM data. Click **Change**.

![Finish connecting: 12 tools, the key sent as hapikey](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/02-hubspot-default-key.png)

5. Set **How it is sent** to **As a bearer token**.

![How it is sent: As a bearer token](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/03-hubspot-bearer.png)

6. **In HubSpot**, create a private app (**Settings → Integrations → Private Apps**; newer accounts find it under **Development** as a legacy app). Give it these scopes: `crm.objects.companies.read`, `crm.objects.contacts.read`, `crm.objects.deals.read` and `crm.objects.deals.write`. Copy its **access token**.
7. **Back in almyty**, click **Create one here**, name it `HubSpot private app`, paste the token and click **Save**.

![The HubSpot private app token](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/04-hubspot-token.png)

8. Do the same for contacts and deals, with these links. This time, after **Change → As a bearer token**, pick **HubSpot private app** from the list instead of creating a new one.

| Part | Link to paste |
|---|---|
| Contacts | `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HubSpot/HubSpot-public-api-spec-collection/main/PublicApiSpecs/CRM/Contacts/Rollouts/424/v3/contacts.json` |
| Deals | `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HubSpot/HubSpot-public-api-spec-collection/main/PublicApiSpecs/CRM/Deals/Rollouts/424/v3/deals.json` |

**APIs** now lists HubSpot's three parts next to Gmail.

![HubSpot Companies, Contacts and Deals, next to the Google APIs](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/05-apis.png)

## 2. Create the assistant

Open **Agents**, click **Create agent** and pick **Autonomous**.

1. Name it `Biz-dev assistant` and pick a model (**gpt-4o** or another strong one).
2. **Personality & style:**

```
Warm, direct and brief. Writes like a founder, not like a marketer: no buzzwords, no exclamation marks, one clear ask.
```

3. **Instructions** (put in your own target profile and names):

```
You help Maya at Lumen with business development (outreach to new prospects).

Target profile: logistics, trucking or courier companies with 100 or more employees that are still a "lead" in the CRM.

When Maya asks you to find prospects:
1. Search the CRM for companies that match the target profile.
2. For each one, find the decision maker: the COO, a VP or the Head of Operations.
3. Skip anyone Maya has emailed in the last 30 days.
4. Write each person a short, personal email (under 120 words) that mentions something specific about their company, and send it from Maya's Gmail. Every email waits for Maya's approval before it goes out.
5. Add a deal for each company you wrote to, with a note of when to follow up.
6. Write a LinkedIn message for each person for Maya to send herself. Never send anything on LinkedIn.

At every wake, check for replies to your outreach:
- When someone replied, move their deal to "Qualified to buy" and tell Maya what they said.
- When someone has not answered after 3 days, send one short follow-up in the same email thread. It waits for approval too. Never follow up more than once.
```

4. **Memory:** turn on **Remember between conversations**, **Whose memory: This agent's own**, **What it saves: Facts it learns**.

![The assistant's instructions and memory](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/06-agent-instructions.png)

5. **Capabilities → Tools and APIs:** search for and tick these eight:

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `hubspot_companies_post_crm_v3_objects_companies_search_do_search` | finds companies |
| `hubspot_contacts_post_crm_v3_objects_contacts_search_do_search` | finds people at a company |
| `hubspot_deals_post_crm_v3_objects_0_3_create` | adds a deal |
| `hubspot_deals_post_crm_v3_objects_0_3_search_do_search` | finds a deal |
| `hubspot_deals_patch_crm_v3_objects_0_3_deal_id_update` | moves a deal on |
| `gmail_gmail_users_messages_list` | finds emails |
| `gmail_gmail_users_messages_send` | sends an email |
| `gmail_gmail_users_threads_get` | reads an email thread, to see replies |

![The eight tools ticked](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/07-agent-tools.png)

6. Click **Save**, go back and click **Activate**.

## 3. Put it in Slack

You talk to the assistant in Slack, and it posts what it did to your sales channel.

**In Slack**, go to **api.slack.com/apps**, click **Create New App → From scratch**, name it `Biz-dev assistant` and pick your workspace. Then:

- **OAuth & Permissions → Bot Token Scopes:** add `chat:write`, `im:history`, `app_mentions:read` and `users:read`. Click **Install to Workspace** and copy the **Bot User OAuth Token** (it starts with `xoxb-`).
- **Basic Information:** copy the **Signing Secret**.

**In almyty**, open the assistant's **Channels** tab, click **Add channel** and pick **Slack**.

![The Slack channel](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/08-slack-channel.png)

1. Open **Advanced** (one workspace with a bot token) and click **Create one here**.
2. Open **More options**, choose **Bot token**, name it, and paste the **Bot user OAuth token** and the **Signing secret**. Click **Save**.

![The bot token and signing secret](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/09-slack-token.png)

3. Click **Save** at the bottom, then **Publish**. The page shows a **Callback URL**.
4. **In Slack**, open **Event Subscriptions**, turn it on, paste the Callback URL as the **Request URL**, and under **Subscribe to bot events** add `message.im` and `app_mention`. Save.
5. Invite the bot into your sales channel: in that channel, type `/invite @Biz-dev assistant`. Note the channel's ID (open the channel's details; it is at the bottom and starts with `C`).

![The Slack channel, live, with its callback URL](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/10-slack-live.png)

## 4. Keep it working: Always on

On the assistant's **Overview** tab, under **Always on**, click **Set up always on**.

1. **Standing instructions:**

```
Keep the outreach moving. At every wake, check Maya's sent emails for replies from the prospects you wrote to. When someone replied, move their deal to "Qualified to buy" and tell Maya what they said. When someone has not answered for 3 days, send one short, friendly follow-up in the same thread (Maya approves it first). Never follow up twice.
```

2. **What wakes it:** **On a timer**, every `4` **hours**.

![Standing instructions and a timer every 4 hours](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/11-always-on.png)

3. **Talk to it yourself:** **Channel: Slack**, and your Slack member ID under **Your address there** (in Slack: your profile, the three dots, **Copy member ID**). Your messages then go into its standing conversation, and it answers you there.
4. **What it may do on its own:** **Does things, and asks you first only before what you pick below**. Under **Ask me first before**, leave only `gmail_gmail_users_messages_send` ticked. (The HubSpot searches are ticked at first because HubSpot searches with the kind of request that usually changes data; untick them, or every search waits for you.)

![Ask me first before sending an email, and nothing else](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/12-ask-first.png)

5. **Reports:** **Post reports to: Slack**, enter your sales channel's ID, **When: Only when it did something**.
6. Turn on **Always on** at the top and click **Save**.

The Always on card on the Overview tab shows what wakes it, what it may do alone and when the timer fires next.

![The Always on card](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/13-always-on-card.png)

## 5. Talk to it in Slack

In Slack, send the bot a direct message:

```
Find new prospects that match our target profile and reach out to them.
```

It searches HubSpot, picks the decision maker at each company, checks Gmail for anyone you already write to, and drafts the emails. Each email stops and waits: Slack and your notifications say it is waiting for your OK.

## 6. Approve the emails

Open **Approvals**. Each request shows the email it will send: **To**, **Subject** and the full text, like the picture at the top. Read it. Click **Approve**, add a note if you like, and click **Approve** again; or **Reject** to drop it.

![Approve, with an optional note](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/14-approve-note.png)

After the last approval it adds a deal per company to HubSpot and posts a report to your sales channel: who it wrote to, whom it skipped and why, and LinkedIn messages for you to send.

![The reports in Slack: the outreach, and the follow-up check a few days later](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/16-slack-reports.png)

## 7. A few days later

Every 4 hours it wakes and checks your sent emails for answers. When a prospect replies, it moves their deal to **Qualified to buy** and tells you what they said. When three days pass without an answer, it drafts one follow-up in the same thread and asks you first.

![The follow-up waiting for approval](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/15-followup-approval.png)

**What woke it lately**, on its Always on page, lists every wake: the timer, your messages, **Wake now**.

![What woke it lately](/screenshots/tutorials/bizdev/17-what-woke-it.png)

## Make it yours

- **Another CRM.** Any CRM that publishes an OpenAPI description works the same way, for example Pipedrive: connect its description under **APIs**, add its API token, and tick its "search organizations", "search persons", "add deal" and "update deal" tools instead of HubSpot's. Change "deal stage" names in the instructions to the ones your pipeline uses.
- **Outlook instead of Gmail.** Connect Microsoft Graph (see [Make it yours](/tutorials/founders-associate#make-it-yours) in the founder's tutorial) and tick its "send mail" and "list messages" tools; put "send mail" on the ask-first list.
- **Leads from somewhere else.** If your target list lives in a spreadsheet or a lead-data service with an API, connect that instead of the HubSpot company search.
- **Drafts instead of sends.** Give it `gmail_gmail_users_drafts_create` instead of `messages_send`: it leaves each email as a draft in Gmail for you to send.

## Good to know

- **Ask first only applies to Always on.** The **Ask me first before** list holds the emails it sends while it works on its own, including everything you ask it in Slack as "you". If you try it with **Try it** on the Overview tab, or someone else writes to it, it sends straight away. Try it on a test address first.
- **Only you, as "you".** Messages from other people in Slack run as the channel, and the channel cannot use your HubSpot or Gmail connections. The assistant then reports finding nothing.
- **LinkedIn stays manual.** It only writes the messages; you send them.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| HubSpot calls fail with **401** | The key is still sent as `hapikey`. On the API's page, **Key → Change → As a bearer token**. |
| Every HubSpot search waits in **Approvals** | Untick the HubSpot tools under **Ask me first before** (step 4). |
| Saving Always on does nothing | Look for the message in the corner: a Slack report needs a channel ID starting with `C`, not your member ID. |
| It says it found no companies | Check the target profile against your CRM's own values (HubSpot's industries are codes such as `TRANSPORTATION_TRUCKING_RAILROAD`), and that you wrote to it as yourself. |
| A reply it should have seen is not mentioned | It reads Gmail at most every few minutes; the next wake picks it up. |
| Slack shows nothing after you write | Check **Event Subscriptions** in Slack shows the Request URL as **Verified**, and that `message.im` is subscribed. |
| Always on was **paused because the agent woke more often in an hour than it may** | **Wake now** counts too. Wait an hour, or click **Turn it back on**. |
